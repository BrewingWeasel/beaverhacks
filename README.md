## Inspiration

Socialization in college can be difficult, and after the activity-packed first
few weeks of college, it can be a challenge for first-years to continue to
connect with new people. If only there was a way to meet people near you in a
fun, low-stakes environment that you could create at any time.

We also saw that there was no fun and regular way for dorms across campus to
interact. We decided to put them together and add have students test their
cooperative skills to lead their dorm to glory.
